 Measurement(?m) ^ hasMeasurementLocation(?m, ?loc) ^ Incident(?i) ^ isLocationOfIncident(?i, ?loc) -> hasIncidentClimateParameter(?i, ?m)
```

- `hasMeasurementLocation(?m, ?loc)` links the measurement to a specific location.
- `Incident(?i)` represents an incident.
- `isLocationOfIncident(?i, ?loc)` links the incident to the same specific location.
- `hasIncidentClimateParameter(?i, ?m)` associates the incident with the measurement taken at that location.

- `isLocationOfMediaItem(?l, ?m)` indicates that the same location is also the location of the media item.
